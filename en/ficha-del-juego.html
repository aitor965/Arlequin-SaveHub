Each game's page

Everything about a game in one panel: how it runs on your PC, your FPS, your settings and your saves.

Clicking a game opens its page, in three tabs: Overview (your hours, the last time you played, how much space it takes, the requirements, your FPS and your graphics settings), Saved games and Info (your achievements, the description, the store, the studio, the release date, where it's installed and where it saves).

Runs on my PC? GameHub knows your CPU, your graphics card and your memory, and compares them with each game's minimum and recommended requirements on the Steam store. You'll see a traffic light: green if it runs with room to spare, yellow if it meets the minimum and red if it falls short. In the library you can filter to see only the games that run with room to spare, the ones that run or the ones that don't.

And also how many FPS you'll get, with a range (“≈ 75 FPS (65-85)”) and at each quality: Low, Medium, High and Ultra. If you've already played it, your real FPS come first (only the ones from your current GPU and CPU); if not, it's worked out from the community's sessions (if you share yours or have ArlequinPro) or, as an approximation, from the Steam requirements.

What limits you and what's worth upgrading? GameHub works out separately what your graphics card would give and what your CPU can handle, and tells you clearly: “The GPU is the limit: a better CPU would barely raise your FPS”, or the other way round, or that they're evenly matched and you'd have to upgrade both.

It also has its saved games grouped (saves, settings…), its backups and buttons to open its folder or install it.

How to use it

  • Click any game in the library and switch tabs at the top.
  • The “On my PC” filter in the bar at the top of the library.
