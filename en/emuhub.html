EmuHub

In the beta Your console games in the library, opened with their emulator and with community opinions. Windows only.

EmuHub finds the emulators you have (RetroArch, PCSX2, DuckStation, Dolphin, PPSSPP, RPCS3, Ryujinx and more), recognises the console games in your folders and puts them in the library next to your Steam or Epic games, with a filter by console. «Play» opens them with their emulator and counts the hours.

Their saves and save states go into SaveHub just like PC games: backup when the emulator closes, restore and cloud. With ArlequinPro you also get automatic save states every 15 to 60 minutes, without touching yours. In Play Hub they have their own Consoles tab, and GameHub sets up the controller in PCSX2, DuckStation and Dolphin.

Each game has «Opinions»: which emulator and settings work best, voted by the community, and whether it runs well on your PC. GameHub never downloads or shares games or BIOS files: it only uses what you already have.

How to use it

  • EmuHub is in the sidebar, under Hubs.
  • Choose the folder with your console games; it finds the emulators by itself (and if one is missing, it takes you to its official website).
  • Click a game to play it or to see its «Opinions».
  • To write your settings, set a nickname; you don’t need one to say how it runs or to vote.
