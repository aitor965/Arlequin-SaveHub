LootHub: free games to keep

The games stores give away for a limited time, all together, with a heads-up when a new one appears.

LootHub gathers the games that Epic, Steam, GOG, itch.io, Ubisoft, EA and Prime Gaming give away for a limited time and that are yours forever if you claim them in time (no free-to-play games or demos). The list is updated every day and you can see it even without internet.

There’s a tab for each store and filters by genre, by days left and by “Runs on my PC”. Each game opens a page with its description, its requirements and the approximate FPS on your PC. “Get it free” opens it on the web or in the Steam or Epic app, and it can remember your choice. GameHub doesn’t claim anything for you or send anything: it only reads the list.

If you want, it shows a Windows notification when a new one arrives (off by default). It’s free for everyone, no ArlequinPro needed. New ones are also posted on the Arlequin Discord.

How to use it

  • Tools → LootHub.
  • Click a game to see its page and “Get it free”.
  • “Notify me of new ones”, top right, for the Windows notification.
  • The ones you already have show as «Claimed» (if they are in your library or you mark them by hand): they go to the end of the list and have their own filter.
  • Steam giveaways show the date they end.
