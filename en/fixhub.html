FixHub

In the beta Each game's known issues and how to fix them, written by players.

FixHub gathers each game's known issues and the fixes the community has found. If a fix works for you, vote it with “It worked for me” and the most voted ones rise to the top.

You can look at just your installed games or your whole library, and with “Translate” you can read in your language what others wrote in theirs.

How to use it

  • FixHub is in the sidebar, under Hubs.
  • Pick a game to see its issues and fixes.
  • To post an issue or a fix, choose a nickname; reading and voting don't need one.
  • You can delete what you wrote whenever you want.
