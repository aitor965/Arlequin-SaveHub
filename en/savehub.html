Your saves, always safe

SaveHub is the part of GameHub that looks after your saved games: it backs up each save when you close the game and lets you get it back in one click, on your PC or from your own cloud.

Automatic backups

Every time you close a game, GameHub backs up your save. No need to remember.

When you close a game, GameHub makes a compressed backup of its saves in the folder you choose (ideally on another drive or in a folder you already keep in the cloud). It can also back up the game's settings (graphics, controls and keys) and the saves some games keep in the Windows registry.

It never backs up logs or caches (for example, the shader caches many games generate): only what matters.

For games you leave open for hours (MMOs, survival…), with ArlequinPro it can also back up during the session every 15, 30 or 60 minutes.

How to use it

  • It works on its own: there's nothing to set up.
  • “Back up now” on the game's page makes a backup right away.
  • In SaveHub, “Back up everything” backs up all your games.
🛡️

Protection against damaged saves

If a backup comes out wrong, the good ones aren't lost.

If an automatic backup comes out much smaller than the previous one (for example, because the game closed badly or the save got corrupted), GameHub doesn't delete the good backups you already had: it keeps them as well as the new one.

It keeps several backups per game (3 with ArlequinFree; with ArlequinPro, 5 by default and up to 10 or unlimited): when you go over the limit, the oldest one is deleted.

How to use it

  • It's on by default. You can change it in Options → SaveHub.
↩️

Restore and take your backups with you

Go back to any backup in one click, or take them to a USB stick.

On each game's page you see its backups (when they were made and why: when closing the game, by hand…) and you can restore any of them. Before restoring, GameHub keeps what you have now, in case you get it wrong.

With “Copy to…” you take all your backups, the latest one of each game, the ones of games without a launcher or the ones of a specific game to another folder or to a USB stick.

How to use it

  • Game page → Saved games → Restore.
  • SaveHub → “Copy to…”.
🗂️

Save slots

Several saves in games that only let you have one. With ArlequinPro.

Some games have a single save: if you want to start over, or let someone else play on your PC, you lose yours. With save slots you have up to 10 saves per game and switch between them in one click.

A new slot can start from scratch or as a copy of the save you're using. When switching, GameHub first stores the current save in its slot, checks it's fine and, if anything fails, leaves everything as it was. Your game settings don't change.

With your cloud connected, slots are stored there too and you have them on all your PCs.

How to use it

  • Game page → Saved games → Save slots.
  • With the game closed: click “Slot 2” and choose how it starts.
☁️

Your saves in your own cloud

Connect your Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox and take your saves to another PC.

Each backup is uploaded to a GameHub folder in your cloud: the rest of your files are left alone. When you bring a backup to another PC, GameHub adapts it to that PC even if your Windows user has a different name or your games are on another drive.

With ArlequinFree, your backups are uploaded to your cloud from 1 PC (if you change PCs, you can move to the new one once every 30 days) and from any PC you can see what's in the cloud and restore a backup by hand. With ArlequinPro, your saves sync on their own across all your PCs and GameHub warns you if another one has a newer save.

The cloud is yours: Arlequin doesn't keep your saves on any server of its own. You sign in on each service's official page, and GameHub never sees your password.

How to use it

  • In SaveHub, click Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox and sign in.
  • From then on, every backup is uploaded on its own.
🔎

Knows where each game saves

And what it doesn't know, it learns while you play.

Each game keeps its saves somewhere different (Documents, AppData, the game folder, the registry…). GameHub knows from what the stores themselves keep on your PC (for example, Steam's cloud rules) and from the community database.

If it doesn't know, it watches which files the game writes while you play and learns the save folder. It also detects folders shared by several games (like Call of Duty's) so saves don't get mixed up. And you can always add a folder by hand.

How to use it

  • Game page → Saved games → “Add folder”.
⚙️

Your way

Everything in SaveHub is set up in Options → SaveHub.

  • Backup folder (and open it).
  • Backup when closing the game, and include the settings.
  • Protection against damaged saves.
  • Backups kept per game, and backup while playing.
  • Compression: fast, normal or maximum (takes less space, takes a bit longer).
  • “Never back up”: files or folders you don't want in your backups (for example *.mp4 or Screenshots).

How to use it

  • Options → SaveHub.
