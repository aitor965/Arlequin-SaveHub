Your way

Themes, language and how GameHub behaves.

Pick a theme (Arlequin, Midnight, pitch-black Dark or white-background Light; with ArlequinPro, also Sunset and Neon), in English, Spanish or Simplified Chinese. For PCs with few resources there's light mode, which removes animations, transparency and glow (you can already turn it on in the welcome screen).

In the beta, GameHub is also in Russian, Portuguese (Brazil), German, French and Italian.

GameHub can start with Windows hidden in the system tray (to count your hours and back up your saves even if you play from another launcher). It updates itself (if you skip versions, it shows you all the news together) and the first time it shows you your games by store so you can remove anything that isn't a game.

In the beta, in Options → General → Updates you can choose “Stable versions only”.

In Options → Privacy and data you can send the creator of Arlequin an idea or something that's wrong (with your email or Discord if you want a reply), and Arlequin notices appear at the top of the window when there's something important.
