Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026 · Applies to Arlequin GameHub (including SaveHub, its save backup part) · This is a translation of the Spanish version, which prevails in case of doubt.

In short: Arlequin GameHub runs on your PC. It doesn't need an account, doesn't show ads and your saves stay on your computer, unless you decide to upload backups to your own Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox account. The first time you open it you're offered Help improve Arlequin, which anonymously shares save paths, the computer's hardware and, if you turn it on, your sessions' FPS (section 3). The box comes ticked: you can untick it before you start or turn it off later in Options.

1. Who is responsible

Arlequin GameHub is a free program developed by aitor965 (Arlequin). SaveHub, the part that backs up and restores your saves, is included inside GameHub; the former standalone Arlequin SaveHub program is no longer distributed. For any privacy question you can write to arlequinsavehub@gmail.com.

2. Data handled on your computer

To do its job, GameHub reads and stores on your PC:

  • The list of installed games reported by your launchers (Steam, Epic, GOG, etc.), their folders and your hours played.
  • The save folders and, in games that use them, the Windows registry HKEY_CURRENT_USER keys where they keep the save.
  • SaveHub's backups, in the folder you choose.
  • Each game's graphics settings files (and, in Unity games, their registry keys), to show them on its page and, only if you ask with “Save”, change them. Before the first change a copy of the original is kept in GameHub's data folder, so it can go back to how it was.
  • The images you choose as a game's cover, copied into GameHub's data folder.
  • Your computer's specifications (section 3.2), for the Runs on my PC filter and the Your PC section.
  • Settings, caches and logs, in GameHub's data folder (%APPDATA%\com.arlequin.gamehub; it opens from Options → Privacy and data → Your data).

None of this is sent to the developer or to any project server, except what's described in section 3 if you have Help improve Arlequin turned on.

3. Help improve Arlequin (optional)

It's offered in the welcome window with the box ticked: you can untick it before you start, and turn it on or off whenever you want in Options → Privacy and data. Nothing is sent before you've seen that window. On that same page you can choose one by one what you share (section 3.3).

3.1 Save paths

GameHub anonymously sends what it has learned and Arlequin's database doesn't know yet, to compare it with what other computers send and expand that database:

  • Learned paths: the save folders you add by hand and the ones GameHub finds for games with no known path. They're sent as templates with placeholders (for example <winDocuments>/My Games/Game), never with your username or your computer's own folders; if a path can't be anonymised, it isn't sent.
  • Games without a path: the name, store and store ID of installed games the database doesn't know or has no save path for.
  • The GameHub version and a random installation identifier, used only to count how many different computers confirm the same data. It isn't linked to your name, your account or your computer.

The content of a save is never sent. It's sent at most once an hour and only if there's something new.

Game catalogue: to recognise games on other computers (for example, one installed in a folder where its store didn't record it), GameHub also sends the name, store, store ID, install folder name and executable name of the games in your stores, installed or not. It goes separately from the rest: each submission carries a new random identifier, not your installation's, so it can't be known whose list it is. Only what the database doesn't have yet and hasn't been sent before is sent, at most every 5 days or when you click Refresh, and never with a game open.

Steam games on your account: Steam keeps on the PC the games of every account that has signed in on it and those of your Steam Family, without saying whose they are. To tell them apart, GameHub sends the SteamID of the active Steam account to our service (Google Apps Script), which asks Steam which games that account has and returns only the list of game numbers. Neither the SteamID nor the response is stored anywhere of ours; your GameHub keeps the list on your PC for one day. It only works if game details are public on your Steam profile.

Games found while you play: if you open a program that isn't in your library and looks like a game, GameHub recognises it on your computer (by its executable or its folder) or asks you whether it is one when you close it. That happens only on your PC; if you add it, it's treated like any other game in your library.

3.2 Your computer's hardware and FPS

GameHub looks at your computer's specifications for the Runs on my PC filter, which compares your computer with each game's minimum and recommended requirements (the Steam store's). The comparison is done on your computer. With Help improve Arlequin turned on, this data is also sent anonymously, to build hardware statistics of players:

  • CPU: brand, model, cores and threads.
  • Graphics card: brand, model, memory and, on NVIDIA cards, the power limit in watts.
  • RAM: amount, type (DDR4, DDR5...), speed, number of modules, brand, module model and its factory CL latency.
  • Operating system and its version.
  • Drives: how many of each type there are (NVMe, SSD, hard drive) and their total capacity. Never their content.
  • Type of computer (desktop, laptop or Steam Deck).
  • Screens: resolution, refresh rate, brand and model, size in inches, which is the main one and the panel type if you set it (OLED, IPS, VA...).
  • Keyboard and mouse: the model name they report to Windows (for example "Razer Basilisk V3").
  • Internet connection: whether it's wired or Wi-Fi, the link speed and the result of a speed test (download, upload and ping) against Cloudflare's servers (speed.cloudflare.com). The test takes about 25 seconds (several connections at once, to measure fast connections properly) and is never run with a game open: 5 minutes after opening GameHub if the last one is more than 30 days old, or with the button in Options → Your PC.
  • Country, according to Windows' regional settings (never from your IP address or your location).
  • Virtual memory (Windows page file): how much there is and whether Windows manages it.

Your sessions' FPS (separately optional): if you also turn on Measure FPS while you play (Options → Your PC), GameHub uses PresentMon, a free tool from Intel, to know how often the game draws a frame, without touching the game. When you close the game a summary of the session is stored on your computer and, with Help improve Arlequin turned on, it's sent: the game (name, store and store ID), the day, the minutes measured, average FPS, the median, the 1% low, stutters, what was limiting (the GPU or the CPU), the peak RAM, graphics memory and virtual memory used during the session, the screen it was played on, the game's graphics settings (quality, resolution, upscaling, frame generation, ray tracing, VSync and FPS limit) and the CPU, graphics card and memory model it was played with. It's used to work out how many FPS each game gets depending on the hardware.

The computer name, your username, serial numbers and any other hardware identifier are not sent. Hardware is sent the first time and then at most every 30 days (or when you click Refresh, if something has changed), and a single row is kept per installation, which is replaced if you change components.

3.3 Each metric separately

In Options → Privacy and data you can choose what you share: save paths, hardware, screens, keyboard and mouse, internet, country and sessions with FPS. What you don't share isn't sent. You still see your own data in GameHub the same way (Your PC, your FPS and the "Runs on my PC" filter). What's worked out from community data (for example, FPS estimates with other players' sessions, comparisons or statistics) is only shown to those who share that same metric or have ArlequinPro.

3.4 Where it's stored and what it's used for

Submissions are stored in a private project spreadsheet (Google Apps Script). Only aggregated data, without identifiers, reaches the public repository: paths confirmed by several computers, hardware percentages (contribuciones/HARDWARE.md) and FPS medians grouped by game, graphics card, resolution and quality (contribuciones/FPS.md), never individual sessions. For the Statistics filters, the public database also carries an anonymous list of computers with the same grouped values (for example "32 GB", "27"", "600-1000 Mb/s"): without identifiers or country, sorted by content (not by computer) and with each value as it is, even if only one computer has it (they're hardware specifications that don't identify anyone); GameHub doesn't show the result of filters that leave fewer than 3 computers. This data is used in Arlequin's databases and products, current or future, including paid ones. Personal data is never sold or transferred.

You can turn the option off at any time and ask for your submissions to be deleted by writing to arlequinsavehub@gmail.com; to identify them, give the installation identifier found in contribuir.json, inside GameHub's data folder.

3.5 Tester codes

If you ask for a test code, GameHub creates a request file with this computer's ID: a short code worked out from the Windows installation identifier, from which the original can't be recovered. It's only sent if you send that file yourself, and it's used so the code doesn't work on another PC. The code is checked without internet; GameHub only downloads the public list of revoked codes.

4. Use of Google data (Google Drive)

Connecting the cloud is optional and is done in SaveHub → Cloud. GameHub uses the registered application Arlequin SaveHub for this, which is the name you'll see on Google's, Microsoft's and Dropbox's permissions screen. With Google Drive it requests the permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, which only gives access to the files and folders created by the application itself (the Arlequin GameHub folder in your Drive). It can't see or change the rest of your Google Drive.

Google dataWhat it's used for

Files and folders created by the application (ZIP backups and the index sincronizacion.json) Uploading your backups, listing them, downloading them when you restore or change computers and deleting the oldest ones. The index records which backups exist and from which computers (by computer name), to sync your PCs and your Steam Deck.
  • Storage: the access token is stored only on your PC, in nube.json inside GameHub's data folder. It isn't sent to the developer or to third parties.
  • No sharing or selling: Google data is not shared, sold or transferred to anyone, is not used for advertising and is not used to train artificial intelligence models.
  • No human access: nobody on the project has access to your account, your files or your token.
Arlequin GameHub's and its registered application Arlequin SaveHub's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Only one cloud can be connected at a time. To change clouds, disconnect the current one in SaveHub → Cloud.

6. Use of Dropbox data

The application is registered with Dropbox with “App folder” access: it can only use the Apps/Arlequin SaveHub folder in your Dropbox (GameHub uses the GameHub subfolder) and can't see or change the rest of your files.

As with Google: Microsoft and Dropbox data is not shared, sold or transferred to anyone, is not used for advertising or to train artificial intelligence models, and nobody on the project has access to your account, your files or your tokens.

7. How to withdraw access and delete your data

  • In GameHub, SaveHub → Cloud → Disconnect: deletes the token from your PC.
  • You can revoke access at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, Microsoft at account.live.com/consent/Manage and Dropbox at dropbox.com/account/connected_apps.
  • The backups you uploaded stay in your cloud until you delete them.
  • To delete all local data, uninstall GameHub, delete its data folder (%APPDATA%\com.arlequin.gamehub) and your backup folder.
  • To delete what you've shared with Help improve Arlequin, follow section 3.4.

8. This website

arlequinsavehub.com is a static information website: it doesn't use cookies, forms or analytics. It only remembers in your browser the language you choose with the ES / EN button. The hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log technical access data, such as the IP address, under its own terms.

9. Minors

GameHub isn't specifically aimed at minors and doesn't collect anyone's personal data. The use of Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, Discord and Steam is subject to each service's age requirements.
