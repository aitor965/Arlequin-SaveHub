✦ Install

Ready in a minute

Download it

Arlequin-GameHub-Setup.exe, from the official releases page on GitHub.

Open it

As it isn't signed yet, Windows may warn you: click More info → Run anyway.

Check your list

The first time, it shows you the games it has found, by store, so you can remove anything that isn't a game.

Play

Choose where to keep the backups of your saves and, if you want, connect your cloud. From then on it updates itself.

Requirements: 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. To uninstall it, Windows Settings → Apps → Arlequin GameHub. Your save backups stay in their folder.

✦ FAQ

What people usually ask

Is it really free?

Yes. ArlequinFree is free forever and with no ads. ArlequinPro, when it's out, will be optional. See the difference in Free and Pro.

Does it ask for my Steam, Epic… passwords?

No, never. GameHub reads the games you already have on your PC and opens each game with its own launcher. For the cloud, you sign in on Google's, Microsoft's or Dropbox's official page, and GameHub doesn't see your password.

Windows says the program isn't recognised

That's normal while GameHub is in beta and isn't signed yet. Click More info → Run anyway.

Why doesn't it find a game?

Click “Find games” so it checks your stores again. If it's a game without a launcher, add it with “Add game” or tell it in Options → Library which folder holds your loose games.

Why doesn't it find a game's saves?

The database is just getting started. You can add the folder by hand on the game's page, or simply play: GameHub learns where it saves and, if you share, that game will be found on its own on other people's PCs.

What data does it collect?

Only if you leave “Help improve Arlequin” on: save paths, your PC's hardware and, if you measure FPS, the summary of your sessions, always anonymously. In Options → Privacy and data you choose what you share. It's all in the privacy policy.

The HUD or crosshair doesn't show in a game

Set the game to borderless/windowed fullscreen or a borderless window (they don't show in exclusive fullscreen), check they're turned on for that game and that you haven't hidden them with Ctrl+Alt+H or Ctrl+Alt+X.

The HUD shows “—” for temperatures

That data needs a sensor program open: HWiNFO (with “Shared Memory Support”, which switches off every 12 hours in the free version), LibreHardwareMonitor (with its Remote Web Server) or MSI Afterburner. HWMonitor doesn't work, because it doesn't share its data. More in HUD and crosshair.

My FPS don't show up

Turn on measuring in Options → Your PC. The first time, GameHub downloads PresentMon and may ask for permission to read Windows performance data (you need to sign out and back in once).

Does it cause trouble with anti-cheats?

GameHub doesn't get inside any game: it measures FPS from outside and the HUD and crosshair are transparent windows on top of the game. Even so, in tournaments and some competitive matches external crosshairs may be banned by their rules.

Will it be on the Microsoft Store?

Yes, soon. In the meantime it's downloaded from this website and updates itself.

Does it work on Steam Deck or Linux?

Not yet: right now it's Windows only. Linux and Steam Deck are on the way; see What's new.

Is it available in my language?

GameHub is in English and Spanish, and it picks the one from Windows. More languages will come with the first stable versions.
