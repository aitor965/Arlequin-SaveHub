1. What Arlequin GameHub is

Arlequin GameHub is a launcher for Windows that brings the games from your launchers together in a single library. It includes SaveHub, which finds, backs up and restores your games' saves and settings. By downloading or using it you accept these terms.

Always keep additional backups of what matters to you.

4. What it does on your computer

  • Reads the installed games from your launchers and opens them when you ask.
  • Reads, backs up and, when restoring, replaces your games' save folders and, in those that keep their save there, their HKEY_CURRENT_USER keys in the Windows registry.
  • If you turn it on, measures your sessions' FPS with PresentMon (from Intel), without modifying the game.
  • Updates itself when there's a new version.

5. User responsibility

  • Close the game before restoring its saves.
  • Check that the backup folder has space and is a safe place.
  • Use GameHub only with your own games and data.
  • Bear in mind that some online games keep progress on their servers; SaveHub can't back up that progress.
