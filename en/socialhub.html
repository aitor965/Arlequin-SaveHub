SocialHub

In the beta Your Twitch, YouTube and Kick channels inside GameHub, no account needed.

SocialHub follows the Twitch, YouTube and Kick channels you choose, without signing in anywhere, and lets you know when they go live or upload a video. You watch them inside GameHub, with each site’s official player.

Plus, «Live with your games» shows who is streaming the games you have installed right now, so you can discover new channels. Your channel list is stored only on your PC.

How to use it

  • SocialHub is in the side bar, under Hubs.
  • Paste the channel link or type its name and pick the platform.
  • Each channel’s bell sets whether it notifies you; for X only the link is kept.
  • If you remove it from the side menu, SocialHub checks nothing.
