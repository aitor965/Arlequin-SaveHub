✦ 26.10.7

What changes from 26.10.6 to 26.10.7

  • AimHub: new. Warm up your aim in a 3D room with several modes, using your game's sensitivity and field of view, test your crosshair and compete in each mode's Hall of Fame.
  • ReplayHub: new. Save what you just played with Alt + F10 and record manually with Alt + F9; in "Your clips" you can watch them, hear each track separately and, with Pro, trim them. In Free, clips of up to 5 minutes. Also: "Always record" (Pro) records every session of the games you choose, and in game you see whether it is recording, free space and "Clip saved".
  • LootHub: new. Games that stores give away for a limited time, whether they run on your PC, when they end, an "Owned" mark and optional alerts for new ones.
  • DiskHub: new. How much space each game and each folder takes on each drive, and leftovers from uninstalled games.
  • Library: new stores (Riot, HoYoPlay, Wargaming, Ankama and Gameforge), Dofus characters by server, Steam and Epic download progress on the card, console games in their own section, and hours are no longer lost if the power goes out.
  • SaveHub: choose how many backups to keep, it detects if the game closed badly and lets you go back to the previous backup, warns about games with no backup this month, and backups, restores and cloud are safer.
  • Play Hub: controller menu (search, filter, game page, shut down or sleep the PC), rows you can hide and reorder, and two different controllers at once.
  • HUD and crosshair: import and copy CS2 and VALORANT crosshairs, "Always on screen", the game's FPS also while streaming, and battery for more headsets and mice.
  • BenchHub: each value explains what it measures (ⓘ button), it looks better on wide screens and tests on integrated graphics now count. When comparing tests, each chart appears under its section.
  • Your PC: the internet test is faster and has "Stop", and RAM, drives, integrated graphics and keyboards are recognised better. It reads Intel Arc and Iris Xe graphics (temperature, power, MHz…), reads the graphics card Windows uses if you have integrated and dedicated, and AMD watts no longer jump around.
  • Statistics: Games tab by month, "My summary", filter by where GameHub was downloaded, RAM at stock and with OC separately, and drives by model.
  • Chat: it has its own section in the menu and works better with Discord and TeamSpeak.
  • POTATO MODE: GameHub comes back when you close the game, option to turn it on when opening a game, and "Always start in POTATO MODE+".
  • Options: GameHub in Russian, Portuguese (Brazil), German, French and Italian, and "GameHub backup" saves your options and playtime (also in your cloud).
  • Update: "Stable versions only" option, it checks the signature before installing and does not update while ReplayHub is recording.
  • General: GameHub opens faster, the Hubs are grouped in the side menu with a "Bugs and suggestions" button, and when uninstalling you choose what to delete.
