RemoteHub

In the beta Control GameHub and your PC from your phone, over your home network.

RemoteHub turns your phone into a remote for GameHub and your PC: switch scenes, start or stop the stream and the recording, make clips, see the preview and your PC status (FPS, temperatures and usage), open a game from your library or shut down the PC from the sofa.

With movies and videos on your PC it works like a remote control: pause, ±10 s and volume. You can also use your phone as a mouse and keyboard, create your own buttons and play on your PC with a controller connected to your phone. Everything stays on your home network: nothing goes out to the internet or through Arlequin.

How to use it

  • RemoteHub is in the GameHub sidebar. It stays off until you turn it on.
  • Scan the QR code with your phone: on Android, with the Arlequin RemoteHub app; on iPhone, from the browser.
  • Your phone must be on the same network as your PC.
  • Mouse and keyboard are off by default and blocked while a game is open. To play with a controller connected to your phone you need the GameHub from this website.
