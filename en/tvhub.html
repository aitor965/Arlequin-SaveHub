TVHub

Watch or play on your TV at home from your PC, without cables or internet.

TVHub sends your PC's screen to the TV at home. “Play on the TV” uses Miracast with low delay, to play with a controller; “Watch on the TV” gives more quality, for videos and films.

If the TV has no Miracast, you open an address in its browser or send it via Chromecast (Google TV and Android TV). Everything stays on your home network, without internet and without installing anything on the TV. It also works with Xbox Series X|S, with its free Wireless Display app.

How to use it

  • TVHub is in the sidebar, under Hubs; there's also a shortcut in Play Hub and in the tray icon.
  • Turn on the TV, open “Screen mirroring” (or Smart View) on it and press “Play on the TV”.
  • Mark your TV as your usual one and next time it connects straight away.
  • To play, the controller is connected to the PC.
